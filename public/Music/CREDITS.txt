may be used freely in commercial projects without attribution. These credits
are kept as a courtesy and for record-keeping.

SOUND EFFECTS
-------------
- Kenney.nl asset packs — all CC0 1.0 Universal (https://kenney.nl):
  - Interface Sounds (100 files)
  - Impact Sounds (130 files)
  - Music Jingles (85 jingles) — used for checkpoint / level-complete / fail
    stings and gameplay feedback
  - RPG Audio (organic foley: chop, creak, cloth, metal pots, book pages, ...)
  - Various other public-domain samples (Fantozzi footstep recordings, etc.)

Kenney releases his assets under CC0; no attribution is required, but we
thank Kenney (kenney.nl) for keeping game assets free.
