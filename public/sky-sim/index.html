📖 HOW TO FLY

Goal

Take off from Runway 36, fly the island, land back under 400 fpm descent. The plane flies like a real Cessna: it needs airspeed to make lift — no speed, no fly.

Controls

Mousefly — move it where you want to go (click the game to capture it)

Takeoff

  1. Flaps 10° (press F once), trim neutral.
  2. Full power (W to 100%), steer centerline with rudder.
  3. At 55 kt ease the mouse up gently — climb at 74 kt.
  4. Above 500 ft: flaps up, power 75%, trim hands-off.

Landing

  1. Downwind: slow below 85 kt, flaps 10° → 20° → 30°.
  2. Final: 65 kt, ~500 fpm down, aiming at the threshold.
  3. Fast? cut power. Slow? add power — never yank the mouse to stretch the glide.
  4. At 30 ft: flare — look at the far end, bleed off sink, touchdown mains first.
  5. Brake below 40 kt, stay on centerline.

Life on the islands

  • Walk: land (or stop) and press K to step out. WASD + mouse, Shift to run. K by the plane climbs back in.
  • Drive: walk up to any town car and press E — joyride, then E/K to hop out. 1 km earns the Sunday Driver quest.
  • Skydive: press J above 200 ft with a parachute from the shop. Steer the fall, canopy opens at 400 ft, walk away from the landing.
  • Shop: walk to the blue-roof Pilot Shop in Harborview, press E — paints, turbo, STOL kit, parachute…
  • Quests: talk to Marta by the main hangars (E) for jobs. They pay cash — track the ◈ marker top-right.
  • Night: townsfolk go home and sleep (Zzz), street lamps and beacons light up. Try a night landing!

Realism notes

  • Stall: past ~15° angle of attack the wing quits — nose drops, controls go mushy. Recover: push the mouse down, full power, level wings.
  • P-factor / torque: at full power and low speed the nose yaws/rolls left — feed in right rudder.
  • Adverse yaw: rolling right yaws you left — coordinate with rudder.
  • Ground effect: she floats in the last few feet — be patient in the flare.
