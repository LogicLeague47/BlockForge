💠 OFFICIAL MODS — by BlockForge Dev

Official mods are made and maintained by the BlockForge team. They are safe, well-tested, and get updates alongside the game. Download a .bfmod below, then install it in-game: main menu → Mods & Addons → Import Mod (or drag & drop it).

🌐 COMMUNITY MODS — from players like you

Community mods are uploaded by the BlockForge community via the form below. They are sandboxed exactly like official mods — a broken mod auto-disables and never harms your worlds — but you install them at your own risk. To make one yourself, see the upload box, or clone any official mod and change the manifest.

📥 HOW TO INSTALL
1
Download a .bfmod (right-click → Save Link As also works).
2
Open BlockForge → main menu → Mods & Addons.
3
Tap Import Mod and pick the downloaded file — or drag & drop it onto the menu.
4
The mod appears in your list, ON. Toggle or Remove it anytime.

🧑‍💻 WANT TO BUILD YOUR OWN MOD?

Mods are plain JavaScript ES-modules exporting a manifest and optional onLoad(), onTick(dt, api) and onUnload() hooks. Start from any official mod file, read the game API in src/mods.js, then upload your creation below. Community mods appear instantly on this page for everyone to download.

Notes: on phones/tablets, downloads land in your Downloads folder — pick that file when the importer opens. Mods are stored locally on your device and never uploaded to us — only mods you explicitly upload via the community box are shared. Uploaded mods are sandboxed client-side.
