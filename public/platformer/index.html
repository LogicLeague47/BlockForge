🖌 How to Paint

  1. Drag with mouse / finger to paint. Release = solid!
  2. Paint costs jam (pink bar). Jam does NOT regrow — every round refills it full. Mid-round, only ✨ sprinkles, stomps & mold-scrubs earn jam.
  3. Keys 1 strawberry=solid, 2 mint=bouncy, 3 choco=sticky climb.
  4. E / right-click erases your strokes + grey Mold walls.
  5. Grey MOLD burns! Don't touch it — scrub it with E from a safe distance.
  6. Stomp Fuzzies! Don't touch spores. Bounce on Mint to reach high jam!
