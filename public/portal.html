📖 How to Play

BlockForge is a sandbox survival game — mine blocks, build anything, fight mobs, and play with friends online. Here's everything you need to get started.

🚀 Getting Started

  • Play instantly — open the game in your browser, no download needed.
  • Create an account to save your progress, keep your skin and play online.
  • Solo worlds — generate your own world and explore from the main menu.
  • Multiplayer — join a server from the in-game server list, or create your own room and invite friends with a link.
  • Enable sound for footsteps, mining and ambient audio — it really adds to the atmosphere.

🪨 Your First Day

  • Punch trees — break wood logs to get planks.
  • Craft a crafting table (4 planks) and place it down.
  • Make a wooden pickaxe and mine stone to get cobblestone.
  • Upgrade to a stone pickaxe — then hunt for coal and iron.
  • Build a shelter before nightfall — mobs spawn in the dark!

⛏️ Pro Tips

  • Break blocks faster by holding the mouse button down.
  • Always carry a torch when exploring caves.
  • Iron armor and a shield make night-time mobs much safer.
  • Use F3 to see your coordinates when hunting for ores.
  • Build a bed to skip the night.

🌐 Online Play

  • Friends — add friends from the friends menu and invite them to your room.
  • Voice chat — press V to open the voice chat menu.
  • Mods & Addons — grab mods from the portal and import them in-game from the main menu.
  • Player links — share your player page link so friends can find you.
