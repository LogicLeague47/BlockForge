2D BlockForge · a LogicLeague game

v1.0 · same blocks, items & recipes as BlockForge · progress saves automatically

✦ How to play

PC: A/D or ◀/▶ move · Space/W jump · 1-9 select · hold Left-click to mine/attack · Right-click to place · E eats held food.

Mobile: ◀ ▶ ▲ buttons · tap ⛏/🧱 to switch mine/place · touch & hold blocks.

Chop wood → planks → sticks → pickaxes. Stone and ores need a pickaxe or they drop nothing. Coal + sticks = torches. Crafting uses the real 3×3 recipes. Click food in your inventory to eat. Zombies burn at sunrise — light up your base!
