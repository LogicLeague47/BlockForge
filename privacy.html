Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Who We Are

BlockForge ("the Game", "we", "us") is a browser-based voxel building and multiplayer game developed by LogicLeague. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and your rights. It applies to the Game played on our own website and on partner platforms such as CrazyGames.

Data Stored On Your Device

The Game stores the following information locally in your browser using localStorage and IndexedDB:

• Your chosen display username and player settings
• Your single-player game worlds (terrain, structures, inventory, progress)
• Custom skin data you create
• A cached login token used to re-authenticate
• Voice-chat preferences (when enabled)

This local data stays on your device. Multiplayer and social features described below transmit data to our servers.

Accounts & Server-Stored Data

If you create a BlockForge account, we store the following on our servers:

• Your username
• Your password, hashed with the scrypt algorithm and a unique salt (plaintext passwords are never stored or transmitted after login)
• Your friends list and direct messages
• Profile data you choose to set (e.g. tag, linked identities)
• Public game-server listings you create

We also process connection metadata such as IP address and approximate location for security, abuse prevention, and to operate multiplayer rooms. We do not sell your personal data.

Multiplayer & Social

When you join a BlockForge server or use social features, the following is transmitted to our backend (and, for peer hosted servers, to that server):

• Your username and selected skin
• Your in-world position, movement, and actions
• Chat and voice-chat audio (peer-to-peer via WebRTC when voice is enabled)
• Account credentials to verify your login

Server operators may log additional connection information. We are not responsible for data handled by third-party servers you choose to join.

Analytics & Advertising

When the Game is played on CrazyGames, the CrazyGames SDK may collect analytics and advertising data under the CrazyGames Privacy Policy. We do not run our own third-party ad networks; any in-game advertising is provided exclusively through the platform SDK. Please review the relevant platform policy for details.

Children's Privacy

BlockForge is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly create accounts for children under 13 without parental consent. If you are under 13, please do not create an account. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

Depending on your location (including GDPR and CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or withdraw consent for its processing. To request deletion of your account and associated server data, use the "Delete Account" option in the game's account menu or contact us. We will respond within the timeframes required by law.

Data Retention & Transfers

We keep account data for as long as your account exists and a short period afterward to meet legal obligations. Data may be processed and stored in servers located outside your country; where required we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Contact

For any privacy request or question, contact the developer at privacy@blockforge.game. You can also reach us through the support channel linked from the game's main menu.
